The MIS reports every founder should read each month

Five short reports that tell you more about the health of your business than a full set of financial statements.

Financial statements are built for lenders, investors and tax. Founders need something shorter: a monthly pack that shows what changed, why, and what needs a decision. A good MIS pack fits on a few pages and is read in fifteen minutes.

1. Cash position and runway

Cash at month end, cash burned or generated during the month, and how many months the current balance lasts at the recent average. This is the first page for a reason.

2. Revenue by line or channel

Revenue split the way you actually run the business, by product, service line, region or sales channel, compared with last month and with budget.

3. Gross margin trend

Margin by line over the last six to twelve months. A slow slide in margin is easy to miss in a single month and expensive to discover late.

4. Receivables and payables aging

Who owes you money and for how long, and what you owe. Overdue receivables are often the quickest cash improvement available.

5. Cost movements that need an explanation

A short list of expense lines that moved more than an agreed threshold, each with a one-line reason. It keeps the conversation on exceptions instead of every number.

If a report doesn't lead to a question or a decision, it doesn't belong in the monthly pack.

Tools like Power BI or Zoho Analytics can refresh these reports automatically from your accounting system, so the pack is ready on the same day every month.
