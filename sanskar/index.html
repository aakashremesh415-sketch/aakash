About

The Books First. Then the Plan.

I've spent 8+ years in finance and accounts: bookkeeping, monthly closes, financial statements, AR and AP, and the MIS reports, budgets and forecasts that management uses to make decisions. I've worked across SaaS, service, eCommerce and product businesses.

I'm a Partner at Renocrew Solutions and a QuickBooks ProAdvisor, and I work with clients on Upwork, where I have a 100% Job Success Score. Before that, as a Senior Executive in Finance & Accounts at Verve Advisory, I led a team of 25, built budgeting and forecasting models, and moved a client's books from Zoho to QuickBooks with minimal disruption.

Based in Dhanbad, India · Working remotely with clients worldwide

What I Do
