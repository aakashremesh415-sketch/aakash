About

Accounting rigour, product thinking.

Most finance problems aren't really about numbers — they're about process. Who owns the reconciliation, where the evidence lives, what happens when a deadline slips. I spend my days on both sides of that: working with clients on the books, and turning what we learn into systems so the next close is calmer than the last.

When an off-the-shelf tool doesn't fit how our team actually works, I build one. That's how Renocrew ended up with its own learning platform, task and compliance tracker, and recruitment pipeline — all running on one shared login.
