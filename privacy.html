Privacy

What this site collects, and why

Short version: only what's needed to arrange a call or answer a message. No cookies, no trackers, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

Booking a call

The booking form asks for your name, work email and company, and optionally your accounting software, how many entities you have and a note about your books. These are used to:

  • email you a confirmation, a calendar invite and the video link;
  • send one reminder about a day before the call;
  • let you cancel or reschedule from the link in your email;
  • let me prepare for the call.

Until the call, your name, email and company are stored encrypted, so the reminder and the cancel link work. They're deleted automatically after the call. The booked time itself is stored without any personal details. When a call has its own Google Meet link, the event in my calendar holds the meeting link only, not your details.

Sending a message

The message form emails your name, email, company (if given) and message straight to me. Nothing is stored on the website; the email is kept in my inbox like any other correspondence.

Who handles the data

  • Vercel hosts the site and stores the encrypted booking details.
  • Titan Email sends and receives the booking and message emails.
  • Google provides the Meet link, when a call has one.

What isn't collected

No cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. Your IP address is used briefly to stop spam (rate limits) and isn't stored.

Your choices

You can cancel a booking at any time from the link in your confirmation email. To ask what I hold about you, or to have an email conversation deleted, reply to any email from me or use the message form.
