Things I would share with a spy (or anyone else)

None of this is secret. These are the eight habits I'd hand to any business owner, or anyone who asked.

  1. Cash in isn't revenue (yet). Paid upfront? It's a liability until you deliver. That's deferred revenue. More on this.
  2. Profit isn't cash. You can be profitable on paper and still miss payroll. Watch both. More on this.
  3. Reconcile monthly, not yearly. One bad month is a 20-minute fix. Twelve is a project.
  4. Spread big prepaid bills. Annual insurance or software? Expense it month by month, not all at once. More on this.
  5. Same close, same order. A written month-end checklist is the fastest close you'll ever buy. More on this.
  6. Fix the chart of accounts first. Migrating messy books into a new system just gives you messy books faster. More on this.
  7. Plan intercompany on day one. Two or more entities? Set up due-to / due-from accounts before money moves. More on this.
  8. Spot when you've outgrown it. When workarounds take longer than the work, it's time to look past QuickBooks. More on this.
