Profitable. And out of cash.

How does a profitable company run out of cash?

The example

You win a client worth $50,000 a month. Delivery costs $40,000 a month. Great deal. But they pay 60 days after each invoice.

Two months in, you've paid your team $80,000, received nothing, and your P&L shows $20,000 of profit.

Why it happens

Profit comes when you do the work. Cash comes when they pay. Growth eats the gap, so the faster you grow on long payment terms, the more cash you need to carry it.

That's why you watch both: the P&L tells you whether the work is worth doing, and the cash forecast tells you whether you can afford to keep doing it.
