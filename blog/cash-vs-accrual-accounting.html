Same coffee shop. Two different Junes.

Same coffee shop. Same month. Two different answers. Both are right.

The example

Your coffee shop caters an event in June. $2,000. The client pays in July.

  • On a cash basis, June shows $0. The money hasn't arrived yet.
  • On an accrual basis, June shows $2,000, because that's when you did the work.

Which one do you need?

Cash tells you what's in the bank. Accrual tells you what you've earned. You need both to run the business well.
