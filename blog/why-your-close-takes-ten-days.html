Why your close takes ten days

Why does closing the books take ten days every month?

Usually it's not the accounting. It's the waiting: bank statements, receipts and approvals, all landing at month-end.

The fix

Simple, if not easy: do a little every week.

  • Match the bank weekly.
  • Chase receipts as they come in.
  • Keep a written checklist, and close in the same order every month.

Then month-end becomes a final check, not a ten-day scramble.
