Accounting, explained plainly

Short explainers from my LinkedIn: one example, one number, no jargon. Most are short videos, written up here with a transcript.

  • Post · 11 November 2026 Things I would share with a spy (or anyone else) Eight accounting habits I'd hand to anyone: deferred revenue, profit vs cash, monthly reconciliations, prepaid bills, a close checklist, a clean chart of accounts, intercompany setup and knowing when you've outgrown QuickBooks.
  • Video · 10 November 2026 Why your close takes ten days A slow month-end close is usually a waiting problem, not an accounting problem. Do a little every week, match the bank weekly and chase receipts as they come in.
  • Video · 9 November 2026 Same coffee shop. Two different Junes. A coffee shop caters a $2,000 event in June and gets paid in July. On a cash basis June shows $0; on an accrual basis it shows $2,000. The difference between cash and accrual accounting in one example.
  • Video · 8 November 2026 Profitable. And out of cash. Win a $50,000-a-month client who pays in 60 days, and two months later you've paid out $80,000, received nothing and shown $20,000 profit. Why profit and cash drift apart when you grow.
  • Video · 7 November 2026 You paid for it. You haven't used it yet. You paid $12,000 for a year of insurance in January, but only $1,000 is a January expense. How prepaid expenses work and why they keep your monthly P&L honest.
  • Video · 6 November 2026 Five signs you've outgrown QuickBooks QuickBooks is a great tool, until the business changes shape. Five signs it might be time to look at the next step, from Excel consolidations to revenue schedules only one person understands.
  • Video · 5 November 2026 Don't move the mess Copying an old chart of accounts straight into a new accounting system wastes most of what you paid for. What to clean up before you migrate: fewer accounts, the right dimensions and a mapping.
  • Video · 4 November 2026 Three companies. Three sets of books. Three companies, one month-end close. How multi-entity consolidation plays out in QuickBooks Online, Intuit Enterprise Suite, Sage Intacct and NetSuite, and why the structure matters more than the software.
  • Video · 3 November 2026 Ticket money isn't revenue (yet) Sold $100,000 of tickets for an event three months away? Until the event happens it's deferred revenue, a liability, not income. A 70-second explainer with a worked example.
