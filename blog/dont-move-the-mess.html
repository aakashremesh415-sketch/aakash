Don't move the mess

The costliest migration mistake I keep seeing? Moving the mess.

A company buys a new accounting system, then copies its old chart of accounts straight across. Hundreds of accounts. A separate account for every customer. "Misc 2" included.

The new system could track customers, departments and locations as dimensions. Instead, the reports look exactly like they did before, and the money spent on the new system buys very little.

Clean up before you move

  • Fewer accounts. Merge the duplicates and the one-off accounts.
  • The right dimensions. Track customers, departments and locations as dimensions, not as separate accounts.
  • A map from every old account to its new home, so history comes across cleanly.

A new system won't fix old habits. The setup does.
