Three companies. Three sets of books.

Three companies. Three sets of books. One month-end close. Here's how that plays out depending on what you're on.

QuickBooks Online

Every company is a separate file, with its own login. Consolidation happens by hand in Excel.

Intuit Enterprise Suite

A real step up. But in my experience, editing an intercompany entry means switching to the consolidated view, and correcting it means going into the entity itself. Back and forth, all day.

Sage Intacct and NetSuite

Multi-entity and multi-location books are far smoother to work in, provided the chart of accounts and dimensions are set up consistently from day one.

The point

The software doesn't fix the structure. The structure makes the software work. If you have two or more entities, set up due-to / due-from accounts before money moves between them.
