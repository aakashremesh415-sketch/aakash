Five signs you've outgrown QuickBooks

QuickBooks is a great tool. Until the business changes shape. Five signs it might be time to look at the next step:

  1. You consolidate more than one company in Excel.
  2. Classes are stretched to track projects, departments, locations and grants all at once.
  3. Approvals happen over email, and nobody's sure who signed off.
  4. You rebuild the same reports in a spreadsheet every month-end.
  5. Revenue schedules live in a spreadsheet only one person understands.

Outgrowing QuickBooks isn't a failure. It usually means the business is growing.
