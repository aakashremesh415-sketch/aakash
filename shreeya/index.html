06
FAQ

Questions Clients Ask

Which accounting software do you work in?

QuickBooks Online (ProAdvisor since 2022), Xero (certified in 2024), Oracle NetSuite, Sage Intacct and Zoho Books, as well as Odoo, AppFolio and Yardi. I connect them to A2X, MyWorks, Bill.com and Dext, and build reporting in Excel, Power Query, Google Sheets and Looker Studio.

Do you work with Shopify and Amazon sellers?

Yes, eCommerce is my main focus. I bring Amazon, Shopify and TikTok Shop sales in through A2X and MyWorks, and reconcile Stripe, PayPal, Square, Klarna, Afterpay, Adyen and Payoneer payouts to the bank.

Can you catch up or clean up books that are months behind?

Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping and cleanups are a large part of my work, and I migrate clients between QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho and NetSuite with zero data loss.

What does your virtual CFO support include?

Budgets, 13-week cash flow forecasts, financial models, monthly MIS packs and KPI dashboards tracking revenue, gross margin, ad ROI and contribution profitability, plus cost reviews. For past clients, cost restructuring and vendor negotiations cut operating costs by 20 to 30%.

Where are you based, and do you work remotely?

I'm based in Mumbai, India (IST, UTC+5:30) and work remotely with clients worldwide, over video calls and inside your own accounting systems.

How do we get started?

Book a free call from this page and walk me through your books and what isn't working. We'll agree on the next step on the call.
