Still doing your bookkeeping once a year?

A shoebox of receipts every April isn't bookkeeping. A real month-end close has six levels: bank feeds, categorizing, reconciliations, accruals, amortization and P&L analysis.

Plenty of business owners send their accountant a shoebox of receipts every April and call it bookkeeping. That isn't bookkeeping. It's a tax-season panic.

A real month-end close has six levels. If you only catch up once a year, you're skipping five of them, and you're making every decision in between on numbers that are months out of date.

Level 1: Bank feeds

Every bank account and every card is connected to your accounting system, so not a single transaction goes missing. If an account isn't connected, its transactions only turn up when someone remembers to upload a statement.

Level 2: Categorize

Every transaction is coded the same way, every month. Payroll is payroll. Software is software. When the same expense lands in a different account each month, your month-to-month comparisons stop meaning anything.

Level 3: Reconciliations

This is how you know the books are actually right. Bank accounts, cards and loans have to match the books to the cent. If they don't, we find out why, before the month is closed, not a year later.

Level 4: Accruals

Some bills haven't arrived yet when the month ends. Accruals record expenses and revenue in the month they actually happened, not when the invoice shows up. Without them, one month looks too good and the next looks too bad.

Level 5: Amortization

Pay for a year of insurance or software upfront, and the cost is spread across all twelve months. Otherwise one month carries a huge expense and the other eleven look cheaper than they really are.

Level 6: P&L analysis

This is what you actually get at the end: your reports by day 10 of the next month, compared with last month and with budget, with exactly what moved and why.

Then it starts again

The point of all six levels is simple: you make decisions on this month's numbers, not last year's. And then the close starts again, every single month, in the same order.

Here's the quick checklist:

  • Bank feeds: every account and card connected.
  • Categorize: the same coding every month.
  • Reconciliations: bank, cards and loans match to the cent.
  • Accruals: costs and revenue in the month they happened.
  • Amortization: big upfront payments spread over the months they cover.
  • P&L analysis: reports by day 10, against last month and budget.
