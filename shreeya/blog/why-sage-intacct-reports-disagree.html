Do your Sage Intacct reports ever disagree?

When Sage Intacct reports tell different stories, the cause is usually dimension sprawl: missing departments, duplicate locations and untagged lines. Five steps fix it for good.

Your P&L says one thing, the location report says another, and the board pack says something else again. In Sage Intacct, that's usually not a math problem. It's dimension sprawl.

Dimension sprawl looks like this: missing departments, duplicate locations and lines that were never tagged at all. Each report slices the same transactions a different way, so each one tells a slightly different story.

The good news: the numbers were never wrong. They just weren't tagged. Fix it in five levels, and every report tells the same story.

Level 1: Map your dimensions

Agree what each dimension value means, and retire the duplicates nobody uses. If "NYC", "New York" and "NY Office" are all locations, pick one and merge the rest.

Level 2: Make them required

Bills, invoices and journals can't post without a department and a location. Making dimensions required at entry is what stops new gaps from opening up.

Level 3: Clean it up, once

Then deal with everything that's already untagged. Tag every line for the year, and reclass anything posted to the wrong place. It's a one-time job, and Level 2 makes sure you never have to do it again.

Level 4: Report by dimension

Build the P&L, the location view and the board pack on the same tags. When every report reads from the same dimensions, they can't drift apart.

Level 5: The close check

Add one rule to your month-end close: untagged lines have to hit zero before the month is closed. That's how you stop the sprawl coming back.

The short version

Tag it once, check it every month, and every report agrees.
